A ball is thrown at 20 m/s, 30° up. How far does it land?
vx = 20 cos 30° = 17.3 m/s
vy = 20 sin 30° = 10 m/s
t = vy / g = 10 / 9.8 = 1.02 s
R = vx · t = 17.3 × 1.02 = 17.6 m
t = 2 vy / g = 2.0
