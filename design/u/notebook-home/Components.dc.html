Rules these boards follow

  • Violet appears only where neb acts or speaks: the notice card, Quick check, "Read it again", checks due, checked ticks and the building spinner. Quiz me, Add course material and Start studying are ink, because the student starts them.
  • One breadcrumb, in the top bar. The title block holds the cover, the name, the meta line and Subject.
  • Tabs are dividers. The active tab is the same paper as the sheet under it; the others are the darker desk tone (--sunk). A tab exists only when its feature applies.
  • Page numbers and prerequisites stay but move out of the way: the prerequisite becomes "after 1" with the full name on hover, and pages sit in a quiet right-hand column.
  • Moments are grouped by source by default, so the source line is shown once per group, not on every row. "Day" grouping is the alternative. "Check against my notes" lives on each moment, not at the top of the page.
  • The old violet mascot blob is gone. The ask pill (bottom right) is unchanged and not drawn here.
