Screen Coach

Ask Mew about anything on your screen. Mew guides you step by step, and you do the clicking.

When Mew sees your screen

  • Only when you ask. Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space and ask, or choose Look at my screen in the tray. Never in the background.
  • One picture of the window you're working in. Step-by-step coaching sees the whole monitor, and Mew tells you before it starts.
  • You'll see it happen. The edges of the screen glow violet and Windows shows a yellow border each time a picture is taken.
  • Never saved. The picture stays in memory, is sent encrypted to Mew's server and Google's Gemini model to answer you, and is then discarded. It is never written to disk or stored in your account.
  • Mew never clicks or types for you. It points the way; you do it.
  • Pause any time from the tray or with Ctrl+Alt+P.

Screen Coach is off

Shortcuts

Use Ctrl or Alt with a key, for example Ctrl+Alt+Space. Shortcuts work only while Screen Coach is on.

These settings stay on this computer and can only be changed here.
