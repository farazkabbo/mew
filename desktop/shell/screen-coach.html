How to use it

  • Hold Ctrl+Alt, say it, let go. Anything: “is this right?”, “explain this”, “rewrite this sentence”.
  • Ask how, and Neb shows you where to click: “how do I add page numbers?”
  • Ask Neb to do it, and it does: “turn on dark mode”, “find my lease PDF”, “rename this to Budget 2026”.
  • Double-tap Ctrl+Alt for Neb's bar: the pen, Select, the mic and Watch.

Catch my slips

Neb speaks up just before you click on a costly slip (new):

  • Reply All to five people or more
  • An email that says "attached" with nothing attached
  • Closing something with unsaved changes
  • Deleting a folder, or more than 20 files
  • A terminal command that deletes or overwrites for good

It works on this PC only. It reads the names of the buttons in the window in front, the words of an email you're writing (only to look for "attached"), and the line you're typing in a terminal. Nothing is sent or saved. The only record is which rule spoke up, in which app, when, and what you chose. It never uses Neb's AI and never spends questions. It never stops a click: you can always carry on. Pause it from the tray menu any time.

What to catch

Privacy

How Neb sees your screen
  • Only when you ask. Hold Ctrl+Alt, ask out loud and let go: Neb takes one picture then. Or use the mic in Neb's bar, or Look at my screen in the tray. Never in the background, except while you have Watch on (below).
  • One picture of the window you're working in. Step-by-step coaching sees the whole monitor, and Neb tells you before it starts.
  • You'll see it happen. The edges of the screen glow violet and Windows shows a yellow border each time a picture is taken.
  • Not saved, unless you save it. The picture stays in memory, is sent encrypted to Neb's server and Google's Gemini model to answer you, and is then discarded. It is never written to disk.
  • Neb draws beside what it explains in violet ink. Screen shares and recordings don't show it unless you turn that on below. While ink is on screen, Neb notices scrolling, switching apps and key presses (never which keys) so the ink can fade when the page moves.
  • Saving to your notebook is up to you. Say “save this”, or circle it and press Ctrl+S: you see exactly what will be saved, with the page's title and link, and nothing is kept unless you press Save. Saved clips are stored privately in your account, like your notes, until you delete them.
  • Checking your notes is up to you too. Say “check my notes”: Neb reads the text on your screen (after hiding private details, never the picture itself), compares it with your notes, and marks what matches, what differs and what's missing. Only short quotes are kept, with what Neb found, in your account; Neb never changes your notes.
  • Draw on your screen to ask about something. Neb's small pointer rides beside your cursor (it only knows where the cursor or your tablet's pen is, nothing you click or type, and that's never saved or sent). Pick Select in the bar and draw around what's confusing: Neb takes one picture of that screen when you finish your first mark, and explains it step by step with ink.
  • Explanations go to your notebook after a few seconds (the picture, your marks and Neb's ink), unless you press Undo. You can choose to be asked each time, or never, under Helpers. Nothing is saved from blocked apps, and nothing hidden is ever saved.
  • The pen in the bar is yours. What you write with it stays on your screen only: Neb never looks at it, and Clear or closing the bar rubs it out.
  • Watch is yours to turn on. Press the eye in the bar and Neb looks at your whole monitor every few seconds while it's on, only when something has changed, to spot a slip in your working and tell you. The edges glow violet the whole time. Press the eye again, pause, or wait 45 minutes, and it stops. The pictures are handled like every other: sent to answer you, then discarded. Holding Ctrl+Alt turns Watch on for as long as you hold: ask out loud and let go, and Neb takes one picture, answers, and Watch goes off again (unless you'd turned it on yourself).
  • Neb types only when you ask it to. Say “rewrite this sentence” or “type a conclusion” and Neb types its suggestion into the document in front of you, at your cursor (over the text you selected). It stops if you switch windows or press Esc, and Ctrl+Z undoes it. It never types into terminals or password fields, and never clicks anything unless you turn on “Let Neb click for me” under Helpers.
  • You choose how much Neb gives away in the bar: Quiet, Hint, Coach, or Answer for the full working, with why each step is done.
  • Pause any time from the tray, or hold Ctrl+Alt and say “Neb, pause”.

What Neb sees

Microphone access is off for desktop apps, so Screen Coach is paused. Turn it on in Windows Settings.

When you ask about your screen, Neb sees

Neb won't look or listen during a call or recording while “Pause during calls and screen recordings” is on. Turn that off to use Neb while you share.

Cover before anything is sent

Cover before anything is sent

Covered text is hidden with a grey box on this PC, using Windows' own text recognition, before the picture leaves it. Text recognition can miss things, so blocked apps, password fields and private browsing windows are never captured at all.

Blocked apps

Neb never looks at these apps. They're matched by program file name (for example outlook.exe). Mail and messaging apps start blocked; unblock any you want help with.

    More

    Updates

    Neb checks by itself and gets each new version ready in the background. It only restarts when you say so, and only installs versions signed by the Neb team.

    Handoff (being built)

    Hand your work to a teammate from the exact moment you stopped: tray menu → Hand off…, or say "hand this off to Sam". To do that, Neb keeps a timeline of the moments you save and of the app in front (with its page and git branch), on this PC only, encrypted, for 24 hours. Blocked apps and private windows are never kept. Nothing leaves this PC unless you press Send in the preview.
