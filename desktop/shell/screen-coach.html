Screen Coach

Ask Neb about anything on your screen. Neb guides you step by step, and you do the clicking.

When Neb sees your screen

  • Only when you ask. Double-tap Ctrl+Alt for the bar at the top of the screen, then turn the mic on, ask, and turn it off (one picture is taken then), or choose Look at my screen in the tray. Never in the background.
  • One picture of the window you're working in. Step-by-step coaching sees the whole monitor, and Neb tells you before it starts.
  • You'll see it happen. The edges of the screen glow violet and Windows shows a yellow border each time a picture is taken.
  • Not saved, unless you save it. The picture stays in memory, is sent encrypted to Neb's server and Google's Gemini model to answer you, and is then discarded. It is never written to disk.
  • Neb draws beside what it explains in violet ink that only you see (it never shows in screen shares or recordings). While ink is on screen, Neb notices scrolling, switching apps and key presses (never which keys) so the ink can fade when the page moves.
  • Saving to your notebook is up to you. Say “save this”, or circle it and press Ctrl+S: you see exactly what will be saved, with the page's title and link, and nothing is kept unless you press Save. Saved clips are stored privately in your account, like your notes, until you delete them.
  • Checking your notes is up to you too. Turn on the mic in the bar and say “check my notes”: Neb reads the text on your screen (after hiding private details, never the picture itself), compares it with your notes, and marks what matches, what differs and what's missing. Only short quotes are kept, with what Neb found, in your account; Neb never changes your notes.
  • Draw on your screen to ask about something. Neb's small pointer rides beside your cursor (it only knows where the cursor is, nothing you click or type). Pick Select in the bar (freehand, a rectangle or an ellipse) and draw around what's confusing: Neb takes one picture of that screen when you finish your first mark, and explains it step by step with ink.
  • Explanations go to your notebook after a few seconds (the picture, your marks and Neb's ink), unless you press Undo. You can choose to be asked each time, or never, below. Nothing is saved from blocked apps, and nothing hidden is ever saved.
  • The pen in the bar is yours. What you write with it stays on your screen only: Neb never looks at it, and Clear or closing the bar rubs it out.
  • Neb never clicks or types for you. It points the way; you do it.
  • Pause any time from the tray, or turn on the mic and say “Neb, pause”.

Your Neb account

Connect Screen Coach to your Neb account so it can answer your questions.

Connecting opens Neb, where you confirm. This computer gets its own sign-in that only works for Screen Coach, kept in Windows Credential Manager. You can sign it out from here, or from Neb's Settings → Signed-in devices.

Shortcuts

Shortcuts got simpler

Every Neb shortcut is two keys at most now. What changed:

  • Ask about your screen: hold Ctrl+Alt and talk (was Ctrl+Alt+Space). Draw around something while you hold them to ask about it.
  • Draw mode for pen and touch: double-tap Ctrl+Alt (was Ctrl+Alt+D).
  • Save to notebook: circle it, then Ctrl+S in the ask bar, or say “save this” (was Ctrl+Alt+C).
  • Check my notes: hold Ctrl+Alt and say “check my notes” (was Ctrl+Alt+N).
  • Pause: the tray, or hold Ctrl+Alt and say “Neb, pause” (was Ctrl+Alt+P).
  • Next step and repeat: → and ← in the caption card (were Ctrl+Alt+. and Ctrl+Alt+,).
  • Summon Neb: hold Ctrl+Alt while Screen Coach is off (was Ctrl+Shift+M).
Anywhere on your PC
Ctrl+Alt twice Show or hide Neb's bar at the top of the screen: the pen and eraser, Select (what Neb looks at: freehand, rectangle or ellipse), the mic (click, speak, click again), Clear and Close. In silent mode it also ends drawing with the keyboard.
Ctrl+Alt held Summons Neb while Screen Coach is off or paused.
Esc Stop, or clear what Neb drew (only while Neb is showing something).
In the ask bar and caption card
Enter Ask (or “explain this”, with nothing typed)
Ctrl+S Save to your notebook without an explanation
→ ← Next step, repeat this step
Space Pause or resume

Only left Ctrl and left Alt count, and only after a moment with no other key pressed, so typing with AltGr (@, €, {) never starts Neb. Neb reads whether the two keys are down, never what you type.

Privacy

Microphone access is off for desktop apps, so Screen Coach is paused. Turn it on in Windows Settings.

When you ask about your screen, Neb sees
Cover before anything is sent

Covered text is hidden with a grey box on this computer, using Windows' own text recognition, before the picture leaves it. Text recognition can miss things, so blocked apps, password fields and private browsing windows are never captured at all.

Blocked apps

Neb never looks at these apps. They're matched by program file name (for example outlook.exe). Mail and messaging apps start blocked; unblock any you want help with.

    These settings stay on this computer and can only be changed here.
