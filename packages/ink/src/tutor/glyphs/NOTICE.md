# Glyph data notice

`hershey.json` is generated by `scripts/build-glyphs.ts` from the Hershey fonts (`futural`,
`greeks` and `mathupp`) as distributed in
[kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts](https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts) at commit
`1356bf2f83d380fcef68c887e88675eb9d445d86`. Four symbols (≈ ⇌ ⇒ ↔) are drawn by the script
itself in the same coordinate system.

The Hershey font data is distributed under the following use restriction, whose acknowledgements
must accompany the data:

> This distribution of the Hershey Fonts may be used by anyone for any purpose, commercial or
> otherwise, providing that:
>
> 1. The following acknowledgements must be distributed with the font data:
>    - The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working at the U. S.
>      National Bureau of Standards.
>    - The format of the Font data in this distribution was originally created by James Hurt,
>      Cognition, Inc., 900 Technology Park Drive, Billerica, MA 01821.
> 2. The font data in this distribution may be converted into any other format _EXCEPT_ the
>    format distributed by the U.S. NTIS (which organization holds the rights to the distribution
>    and use of the font data in that particular format).

`hershey.json` is our own format (not the NTIS one).
