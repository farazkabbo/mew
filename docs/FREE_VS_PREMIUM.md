## The Free Tier Experience

### What's the 5-Hour Session Limit?

It's simple:
- You get 5 hours of usage
- Once you use all 5 hours, you wait 5 hours to get them all back
- Example: Use all 5 hours at 2pm → Wait until 7pm → Get 5 hours back

### Who Is Free Tier Good For?

✅ **Students** doing homework (30-60 min/day)
- You'll rarely hit the limit
- Feels almost unlimited

✅ **Casual learners** (2-3 hours/week)
- Natural breaks let window refill
- Plenty for occasional study sessions

✅ **Trying it out** (evaluating the product)
- Full feature access to test everything
- Can upgrade anytime

### Who Should Upgrade?

❌ **Heavy users** (>5 hours/day)
- You'll hit limits frequently
- Waiting for window refill breaks flow

❌ **Professional use** (tutoring, teaching)
- Need consistent availability
- Can't have downtime

❌ **Voice quality matters** (presentations, content creation)
- ElevenLabs voices are noticeably better
- More natural and professional

## Voice Comparison

### Kokoro AI (Free)
- **Quality**: Good - Clear and understandable
- **Voices**: 8 options (M/F, US/UK accents)
- **Speed**: Fast generation (~100-200ms)
- **Natural**: Sounds like TTS, but good TTS
- **Best for**: Daily studying, practice, homework help
- **Runs on**: Your server (no external API calls)

### ElevenLabs (Premium)
- **Quality**: Excellent - Very natural and expressive
- **Voices**: 100+ professional voices
- **Speed**: Fast generation (~200-300ms)
- **Natural**: Can sound almost human
- **Best for**: Extended sessions, presentations, when quality matters
- **Runs on**: ElevenLabs cloud API

**Analogy**: Kokoro is like Spotify Free (good quality, occasional limits). ElevenLabs is like Spotify Premium (best quality, unlimited).

## Upgrade Triggers

Free users see upgrade prompts when:

1. **At 80% usage** (4 of 5 hours)
   - Gentle reminder
   - "Want unlimited?"
   - Not blocking, just informing

2. **At 100% usage** (limit hit)
   - Must wait or upgrade
   - Shows time until next chunk expires
   - Clear upgrade path

3. **Repeated limit hits** (3+ times/week)
   - Suggests they're a power user
   - "You're using Neb a lot! Maybe Premium?"
   - Special offer consideration

## Pricing Psychology

### Why $15/month?

- **Netflix Basic**: $15.99/month
- **ChatGPT Plus**: $20/month  
- **Spotify Premium**: $11/month
- **Claude Pro**: $20/month

**Neb Premium at $15 = Positioned as essential learning tool, priced like entertainment**

### Why Not Free Only?

- Server costs (AI API calls, compute)
- Sustainable business model
- Premium users fund free tier
- Quality of service (unlimited = better experience)

### Why Not Higher?

- Student-focused product
- Want to be accessible
- Competitive with other AI tools
- Better conversion at $15 than $25
