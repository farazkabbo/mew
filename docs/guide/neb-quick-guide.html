Neb quick guide

A quiet tutor that lives in your notebook. Here is how to use each part.

1Notebooks, chapters and pages

Your desk holds your notebooks. Keep one notebook per class.

  1. On the desk, press New notebook, type your class's name and create it.
  2. Inside it, add a chapter for each topic and a page to write on.
  3. Write on the page as you would on paper. The notebook's name at the top takes you back to its home; the arrow takes you back to the desk.

Press Ctrl K to jump to any page, or say “open physics chapter 3”.

2Write, and let Neb check your work

  1. Open a page and write a line of working.
  2. Neb ticks what is right and circles a slip.
  3. Press Alt C (or say “check this”) to check your work and unfold the margin, where Neb's notes sit beside yours.

3Ask a question out loud

  1. On a page, hold Ctrl Alt.
  2. Ask your question out loud. You can circle the thing you mean while you hold.
  3. Let go. Neb answers out loud and in writing beside your work.

You start with 8 free questions. A follow-up on the same thing does not use one, and your notebooks keep working at zero.

4Quizzes from your course

  1. Open a notebook's home and press Add course material, or press Ctrl O, or drop a PDF on the notebook.
  2. Choose the pages, keep Use as course material ticked, and press Import.
  3. Neb reads it and gives the notebook a Study tab: its topics, short lessons, and Quiz me.
  4. In a quiz, answer on the page. Neb marks it and tells you why.

Turn “Use as course material” off for PDFs that are not for study.

5Review

Review brings back what you have studied and saved, from every notebook, just before you would forget it. Press Review on your desk. A few minutes a day keeps it.

6The island Windows app

The island is the black bar at the top of your screen. It works over any app. Tap Ctrl Alt twice, quickly, to show or hide it.

Button What it does
Select Draw round what is confusing (freehand, a rectangle or an ellipse). Neb takes one picture and explains it step by step, drawing beside it.
Mic Turn it on, ask your question, turn it off. Neb looks once and answers.
Quiet, Hint, Coach, Answer How much Neb gives away. Answer shows the full working, with why each step is done.
Eye (Watch) Neb watches while you work and speaks up when something slips. It stops when you press the eye again, pause, or after 45 minutes. While it is on, hold Ctrl Alt, ask out loud and let go: no need to select anything.
Pen and eraser Write on your screen. Neb never looks at what you write with it.
Music See and control what is playing.
Bin and ✕ Clear your marks, or close the island.

7Things to say Windows app

With the island's mic on:

  • “Save this”: keeps what you circled in your notebook.
  • “Check my notes”: compares the screen with your notes.
  • “Write an essay on cats”, “rewrite this”: Neb types it at your cursor. Esc stops it.
  • “Open the Insert tab”: Neb's own cursor does it, if “Let Neb click for me” is on in settings. Esc stops it.
  • “Where can I sign out?”: Neb's cursor goes there and points at it, without pressing it.
  • “Neb, pause”: stops Screen Coach until you resume.

8Screen Coach settings Windows app

Right-click Neb's icon in the tray, near the clock, to:

  • open Screen Coach settings and turn it on or off;
  • pause it, or pause it for an hour;
  • choose Look at my screen.

Neb only looks when you ask, or while Watch is on. The screen edges glow violet each time. Pictures are used to answer you and then discarded, unless you save them.
